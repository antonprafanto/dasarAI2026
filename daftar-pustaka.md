# Daftar Pustaka

---

## Buku Teks Utama

1. **Russell, S., & Norvig, P.** (2020). _Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach_ (4th ed.). Pearson.
   - Buku referensi standar untuk AI, mencakup semua topik dari search hingga deep learning.

2. **Goodfellow, I., Bengio, Y., & Courville, A.** (2016). _Deep Learning_. MIT Press.
   - Referensi komprehensif untuk deep learning dan neural networks.

3. **Bishop, C. M.** (2006). _Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning_. Springer.
   - Pendekatan probabilistik untuk machine learning.

4. **Murphy, K. P.** (2012). _Machine Learning: A Probabilistic Perspective_. MIT Press.
   - Cakupan mendalam tentang ML dengan fondasi probabilitas yang kuat.

5. **Hastie, T., Tibshirani, R., & Friedman, J.** (2009). _The Elements of Statistical Learning_ (2nd ed.). Springer.
   - Perspektif statistika untuk machine learning.

6. **James, G., Witten, D., Hastie, T., & Tibshirani, R.** (2013). _An Introduction to Statistical Learning_. Springer.
   - Versi lebih accessible dari ESL, cocok untuk pemula.

---

## Buku Pendukung

7. **Géron, A.** (2019). _Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow_ (2nd ed.). O'Reilly.
   - Pendekatan praktis untuk implementasi ML. Sangat direkomendasikan untuk praktik.

8. **Nielsen, M.** (2015). _Neural Networks and Deep Learning_. Determination Press (online).
   - Pengantar intuitif untuk neural networks.

9. **Sutton, R. S., & Barto, A. G.** (2018). _Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction_ (2nd ed.). MIT Press.
   - Referensi utama untuk reinforcement learning.

10. **Mitchell, T. M.** (1997). _Machine Learning_. McGraw Hill.
    - Klasik yang masih relevan untuk konsep dasar ML.

---

## Artikel Penelitian Klasik

11. **McCulloch, W. S., & Pitts, W.** (1943). A logical calculus of the ideas immanent in nervous activity. _Bulletin of Mathematical Biophysics_, 5(4), 115-133.
    - Fondasi teoritis neural networks.

12. **Rosenblatt, F.** (1958). The perceptron: A probabilistic model for information storage and organization in the brain. _Psychological Review_, 65(6), 386-408.
    - Pengenalan perceptron.

13. **Rumelhart, D. E., Hinton, G. E., & Williams, R. J.** (1986). Learning representations by back-propagating errors. _Nature_, 323(6088), 533-536.
    - Memperkenalkan backpropagation.

14. **Vapnik, V., & Cortes, C.** (1995). Support-vector networks. _Machine Learning_, 20(3), 273-297.
    - Pengenalan Support Vector Machine.

15. **Breiman, L.** (2001). Random Forests. _Machine Learning_, 45(1), 5-32.
    - Pengenalan Random Forest.

16. **Hochreiter, S., & Schmidhuber, J.** (1997). Long short-term memory. _Neural Computation_, 9(8), 1735-1780.
    - Arsitektur LSTM untuk sequential data.

---

## Artikel Penelitian Modern

17. **Krizhevsky, A., Sutskever, I., & Hinton, G. E.** (2012). ImageNet classification with deep convolutional neural networks. _Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems_.
    - AlexNet yang memulai revolusi deep learning.

18. **Kingma, D. P., & Ba, J.** (2015). Adam: A method for stochastic optimization. _ICLR_.
    - Optimizer Adam yang populer.

19. **Vaswani, A., et al.** (2017). Attention is all you need. _Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems_.
    - Arsitektur Transformer.

20. **Chen, T., & Guestrin, C.** (2016). XGBoost: A scalable tree boosting system. _KDD_.
    - Pengenalan XGBoost.

---

## Etika dan Regulasi AI

21. **Barocas, S., Hardt, M., & Narayanan, A.** (2019). _Fairness and Machine Learning_. fairmlbook.org
    - Referensi komprehensif untuk fairness dalam ML.

22. **O'Neil, C.** (2016). _Weapons of Math Destruction_. Crown.
    - Dampak sosial algoritma.

23. **Russell, S.** (2019). _Human Compatible: Artificial Intelligence and the Problem of Control_. Viking.
    - AI Safety dan alignment.

24. **European Commission.** (2021). _Proposal for a Regulation on Artificial Intelligence (AI Act)_.
    - Dokumen regulasi AI terpenting.

25. **Mehrabi, N., et al.** (2021). A survey on bias and fairness in machine learning. _ACM Computing Surveys_, 54(6), 1-35.
    - Survey komprehensif tentang bias.
